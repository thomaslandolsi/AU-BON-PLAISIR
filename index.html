Le petit voyage

Since 2014, the family way.

Au Bon Plaisir started in April 2014 as a small deli by Ledra Street, making sandwiches and quiches. Today it's a family-run brasserie on a quiet side street near the Oxi roundabout.

Gatienne runs the room. The kitchen cooks everything on site: local produce whenever Cyprus has the best, French imports when it doesn't.
